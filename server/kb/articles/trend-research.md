- **Weekly trend digest:** every Thursday, all clients. Covers trending formats, audio, and topics relevant to the client's industry.
- **Custom trend brief:** a short brief for a specific launch or moment. 2 business days from request. Included for gold and platinum, billed for standard.
- **Campaign concepts:** the account strategist proposes 3 concepts per brief. Allow 5 business days for a launch campaign.
- Trend recommendations are checked for brand safety before they are proposed.

To start a brief, MMC needs the launch date, goal, target audience, and any must-avoid topics.
