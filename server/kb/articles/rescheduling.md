---
id: kb-rescheduling
title: Changing, moving or cancelling a scheduled post
type: article
tags: reschedule move change time cancel edit caption swap image
---
- More than 2 hours before the publish time: the coordinator can move, edit or cancel the post in the scheduler after the client confirms in writing.
- Less than 2 hours before: the coordinator asks Social Ops. Changes are best effort and may not land before the post goes out.
- Caption and image edits after approval require re-approval in the Approval Portal unless the client explicitly waives it in writing.
- Cancelling a post that is part of a paid partnership needs the account director's sign-off because of contractual delivery dates.

Confirm the final time back to the client in their local time zone.
