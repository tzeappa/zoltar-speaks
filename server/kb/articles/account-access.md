title: Account access and 2FA for managed accounts
type: article
tags: login password 2fa two factor access permissions reconnect token vault locked out
---
- MMC stores credentials for managed accounts in an encrypted vault. Coordinators never ask for or send passwords by email or chat.
- If the client changes a password or 2FA, the publishing connection may break. The client reconnects the account through the Approval Portal.
- Resetting 2FA or changing the recovery email requires a video call with the verified account owner.
- Adding team members to an account needs written confirmation from the account owner.

If a client shares a password in a message, tell them to change it and do not repeat it in the reply.
