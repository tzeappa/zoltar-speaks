Report numbers and in-app numbers often differ. Usual reasons:

- **Data delay.** Platforms finalize metrics 24 to 48 hours after posting. Reports pull finalized numbers, the app shows live estimates.
- **Definitions.** Reach counts unique accounts; impressions and views count every display. Platforms changed some definitions in the past year; the report glossary uses MMC's definitions.
- **Time zone.** Reports use the client's contract time zone. The app uses the viewer's device time zone, so day boundaries can shift posts between days.
- **Scope.** Reports exclude paid promotion unless the client has the paid media add-on.

If the gap is larger than 20 percent after 48 hours, or the dashboard shows no data for a day, it may be a pipeline problem. Tell Social Ops before replying with an explanation.
