All content goes through the Approval Portal before it is scheduled.

- MMC uploads drafts to the portal at least 3 business days before the publish date.
- The client or their named approver approves, comments, or rejects each post.
- Approval cutoff: 24 hours before the scheduled time. Posts not approved by the cutoff are held, not published.
- Platinum clients can approve up to 2 hours before the scheduled time.
- Approved posts can still be edited or moved up to 2 hours before publishing. Inside 2 hours, changes go through Social Ops and are best effort.

Approvers are set per client in the contract. Adding or removing an approver needs written confirmation from the account owner.
