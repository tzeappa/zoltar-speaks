A takedown removes or archives live content. It cannot always be undone, so it follows a fixed procedure.

- **Who can request:** the account owner or a named approver. Requests from anyone else need confirmation from the owner.
- **Confirm in writing** which post, and whether to archive (recoverable on Instagram and Facebook) or delete.
- **Social Ops executes**, not the coordinator. Target: 30 minutes for platinum clients, 2 hours for others, 24/7.
- A copy of the post, caption, and metrics is saved before removal.
- If the post is part of a paid partnership, the account director is notified because of contractual obligations.

Coordinators acknowledge the request, confirm the details, and hand off to Social Ops. Do not tell the client the post is down until Ops confirms.
