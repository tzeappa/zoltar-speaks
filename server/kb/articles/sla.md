First response targets:

| Tier | Business hours | After hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platinum | 1 hour | 1 hour |
| Gold | 4 hours | Next business morning |
| Standard | 1 business day | Next business day |

Urgent issues (account compromise, takedown, press, active outage) get a response within 30 minutes for every tier, 24/7, via Social Ops.

Business hours are 08:00 to 20:00 in the client's contract time zone, Monday to Friday.
