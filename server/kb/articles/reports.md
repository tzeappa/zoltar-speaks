- **Weekly report:** delivered every Monday by 10:00 client time, covering the previous Monday to Sunday.
- **Monthly report:** delivered by the 5th business day of the month, with commentary from the account strategist.
- **Reporting Dashboard:** updated daily by 08:00 UTC with the previous day's finalized data.
- **Custom reports:** 3 business days from a confirmed brief. Platinum clients get 1 business day.
- **Exports:** CSV and PDF exports are available from the dashboard at any time.

Corrections to a delivered report are reissued within 2 business days once the cause is confirmed.
