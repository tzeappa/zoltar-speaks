MMC moderates comments on managed accounts during business hours, and 24/7 for platinum clients.

- **Hide** is the default for abusive, spam or off-topic comments. Hidden comments stay visible to the author, which avoids escalation.
- **Delete** only for illegal content, doxxing, or explicit client instruction.
- **Block** accounts that post repeated abuse after hiding.
- Keyword filters are set per client and reviewed monthly. Clients can request additions at any time; they apply within 4 business hours.
- MMC never argues with commenters or replies to criticism without an approved response.

Report threats of violence or self-harm to Social Ops immediately.
