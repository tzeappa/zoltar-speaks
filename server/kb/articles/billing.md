type: article
tags: invoice billing payment price scope contract renewal cancel cancellation refund
---
- Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month, payable within 30 days.
- Scope changes (new platforms, extra posts, paid media) are agreed with the account director and confirmed in a contract amendment.
- Renewals are discussed 60 days before the contract end date.
- Cancellation requires 30 days written notice as set out in the contract.
- Coordinators do not negotiate prices, discounts, refunds or cancellation terms. They acknowledge and hand off to the account director.
