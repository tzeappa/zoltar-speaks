title: Press inquiries and crisis communication
type: article
tags: press journalist reporter media statement crisis controversy pr interview
---
- MMC never speaks to press on a client's behalf without an approved statement.
- Any press contact, or any request for a public statement, goes to the PR lead within 1 hour, 24/7.
- The PR lead drafts a holding statement and agrees it with the client before anything is published.
- Coordinators do not confirm, deny or comment on facts of the story, even to the client, until the PR lead is involved.
- During an active crisis, scheduled posts for the client are paused by Social Ops pending PR review.

What to tell the client: the request was received, the PR lead has been notified and will contact them directly, and nothing will be published without their approval.
