A wave is more than 50 hostile comments in an hour, or any coordinated harassment.

1. Social Ops switches the account to heightened moderation: stricter keyword filters and hiding at a lower threshold.
2. Ops checks whether the wave is tied to a post, a news story, or a creator dispute, and shares a short summary with the coordinator.
3. If the wave is about the client's own conduct or statements, it becomes a Brand Safety case and the PR lead is involved.
4. Comments can be limited or turned off on a post only with the client's approval.

Tell the client what MMC is doing now and that no public response will be made without their approval.
