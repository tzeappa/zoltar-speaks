MMC manages Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn, YouTube, and Facebook, plus the MMC Reporting Dashboard and Approval Portal.

Snapchat, Pinterest, Twitch and Reddit are not managed. MMC can include them in a custom strategy engagement, scoped by the account director.

Adding a platform to an existing contract is a scope change.
