title: Suspected hacked or compromised account
type: article
tags: hacked compromised suspicious login password reset unknown posts locked out security breach
---
Signs: password reset emails the client did not request, posts or DMs the client did not send, login alerts from unknown locations, being locked out.

This is always urgent. Social Ops takes over within 30 minutes, 24/7.

What Ops does: pauses all scheduled posts for the account, secures the account through the platform's recovery process with the verified owner, reviews recent activity, and removes unauthorized content after the client confirms.

What to tell the client: do not click links in the reset emails, Social Ops is taking over and will call them, and publishing is paused until the account is secure.
