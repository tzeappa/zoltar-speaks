type: runbook
tags: brand safety backlash controversy hostile comments viral negative press client crisis
---
Trigger: a surge of hostile comments, negative press, or a viral post about a client.

1. Switch the client's accounts to heightened moderation.
2. Pause the client's scheduled posts for the next 24 hours pending PR review.
3. Capture screenshots and links of the most-shared negative content.
4. Notify the PR lead and the client's coordinator with a 3-line summary.
5. Do not post, reply, or delete anything publicly until the PR lead approves.
